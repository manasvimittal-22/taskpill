TaskPill (portable)
===================

This folder is TaskPill without an installer. Nothing is written outside it,
except TaskPill's own settings (%APPDATA%\TaskPill) and, when you click
Connect, a few hooks in Claude Code's settings (~/.claude/settings.json; a
backup is made first).

Start:      double-click TaskPill.exe, then click Connect.
Move it:    you can move this folder; start TaskPill.exe once from the new
            place and it updates its hooks.
Updates:    TaskPill tells you when a new version is out and opens the
            download page.
Remove it:  right-click the TaskPill tray icon -> Disconnect from Claude Code,
            quit TaskPill, then delete this folder.

Windows may warn "Windows protected your PC" because TaskPill isn't signed
yet: click More info -> Run anyway.
